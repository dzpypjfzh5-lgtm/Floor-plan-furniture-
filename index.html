Rooms & doors

Every room the plan puts a number on is built at exactly that number — those are the advertised, measured figures. The drawing was only used for the layout, since tracing it came out 0.1–0.75 m short almost everywhere. Rooms with no printed dimension (bath, laundry, hall, and the wrap-around paving) are marked sized to fit — they take up the slack and are the least reliable.

Resizing a room stretches that room and its fixtures only — it does not push the neighbouring rooms out of the way, so a big change will make rooms overlap on screen.
